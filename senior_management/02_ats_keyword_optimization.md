## Formatting rules that affect ATS parsing (applied in all three resumes)
- No tables for core content — single-column layout with clear section headers (`PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY`, `CORE COMPETENCIES`, `PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE`, etc.) in plain text, parsed reliably by Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Taleo, and iCIMS — the four systems most GCC MNCs run.
- No photo, icons, or text boxes embedded in the parseable body — these frequently get dropped or scrambled by ATS parsers (kept in a separate section of the README on photo strategy).
- Dates in a consistent `Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY` format throughout — the format mismatch between your LinkedIn and CV dates (flagged in the diagnostic report) should be fixed here too.
- Job titles and company names on their own line, not run into the bullet text — this is what most ATS parsers use to segment the experience section correctly.
- Standard section names — avoid stylized headers like "Areas of Expertise" in favor of "Core Competencies" / "Skills," which match ATS field-mapping dictionaries more reliably.

## Keyword density guidance
Aim for each priority keyword to appear **once in the summary and once more organically in a bullet** — twice is enough for ATS relevance scoring; beyond that it reads as stuffing to a human reviewer. All three resumes in this folder follow that rule.
