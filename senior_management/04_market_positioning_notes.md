# Market Positioning Rewrite
### Covers: The Market Positioning Rewrite — hiring style of target firms

## How these firms typically read a senior sales/channel candidate

**Eaton, Schneider Electric, ABB, Legrand, Hager (electrical infrastructure / power distribution)**
- Hiring language leans heavily on: channel partner management, distributor performance, specification-driven project business, EPC/consultant engagement, "commercial excellence," pricing governance, and increasingly "digitalization of the channel" (partner portals, CRM discipline).
- These firms promote from strong distributor-management track records more than from pure direct-sales backgrounds — your entire OBO Bettermann history (earthing, lightning and surge protection, industrial controls, distributor network build-out) maps almost one-to-one onto this hiring pattern. **This is your strongest natural fit** — the "Electrical Infrastructure" resume variant leads with this.
- These are mostly US/European-HQ'd multinationals with formal, matrixed reporting lines; resumes that show board/HQ-level reporting exposure (your "Management Board in Germany" line) land well because it signals you already know how to operate in that structure.

**Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, Honeywell (Energy/Sustainability division) — energy transition / renewables**
- JDs here foreground decarbonization, green hydrogen/ammonia, industrial electrification, and "Transformation of Industry"-style narratives, but the actual day-to-day commercial skill set requested (regional bookings, distributor/partner ecosystems, CRM-led forecasting, compliance/LoA governance) is the *same skill set you already have* — the differentiator they're screening for is genuine curiosity and credentialing in the energy-transition space, not direct prior tenure in it (most candidates moving into this space are also pivoting from adjacent industrial sales roles).
- **Be honest about the shape of this fit**: you have real certification and self-directed knowledge in Green Hydrogen/Renewable Energy, but not direct sales tenure in that specific product category. The strongest, most credible pitch is "engineering + channel-sales leader who has deliberately built energy-transition credentials and wants to apply a proven commercial playbook to a growing sector" — not "already an energy-transition specialist." The former is a compelling, differentiated story or a hiring manager to buy; the latter invites a pointed follow-up question you can't fully back up yet.
- Practical next step if you're serious about this track: a named, verifiable certificate (see the open question in the Impact Statement Rebuilder file) will do more for this pitch than any amount of resume wordsmithing.

**One sector-agnostic "master" positioning**
- For roles or firms where you're not sure which story to lead with (or for recruiters/headhunters who'll be matching you against multiple open mandates), the master resume leads with the underlying, sector-transferable capability: multi-country channel leadership, distributor P&L stewardship, CRM-governed forecasting, and board-level reporting exposure — a profile that reads as credible for a Regional Sales Director, Country Manager, Channel Director, or Head of BD mandate at almost any industrial B2B manufacturer with a distribution-led go-to-market model in the GCC.

## Titling guidance across your four target titles
All four titles you're targeting (Regional Sales Director, Country Manager/GM, VP/Head of BD, Channel/Distribution Director) describe the same underlying scope viewed from a different angle — not four different skill sets. Rather than building four resumes, each resume in this folder uses one strong header, with three alternate header lines noted in the README so you can swap the top line in under a minute to match how a specific job posting titles the role, without touching anything else in the document.
